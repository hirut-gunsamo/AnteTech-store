
# AnteTech Store — Retail Management System

A full-stack, multi-store retail management application for mobile phones and accessories businesses.

### 🌐 Live Application
**[Visit AnteTech Store](https://antetechstore.com/)**

## 📌 Project Overview

AnteTech Store is a centralized business management system designed to simplify retail operations, inventory tracking, sales management, stock distribution, and financial reporting across multiple store locations.

The platform provides a modern, responsive dashboard for business owners and sales employees.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 📊 Dashboard & Reporting
- Sales and inventory summaries
- Store-level performance monitoring
- Daily sales reporting
- Profit and loss reporting
- Financial transaction tracking

### 📦 Inventory Management
- Mobile phone and accessories inventory
- Product category, type, and model management
- IMEI and serial number tracking
- Stock availability monitoring
- Stock transfers between stores

### 🛒 Sales Management
- Sales transaction recording
- Cash and bank transfer tracking
- Sales history and corrections
- Automatic inventory updates

### 🏢 Multi-Store Management
- Centralized main store inventory
- Multiple selling locations
- Stock requests and approval workflows
- Delivery and receipt tracking

### 💰 Financial Management
- Bank deposit tracking
- Expense management
- Payroll and commission management
- Profit and loss reports

### 🔐 Security
- User authentication
- Role-based access control
- Protected application routes
- Session management
